Learning outcomes

By the end of the module, students should be able to:

  • Critically examine the project processes behind an enterprise IT delivery challenge.
  • Integrate evidence from multiple sources to analyse a project management issue.
  • Assess which management methods and tools fit the project and its business constraints.
  • Explain the legal, ethical, social and professional issues facing IT project managers.
