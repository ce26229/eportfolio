About the module

This module introduces core security and governance principles based on mapping information security and risk management roles onto security models. You’ll gain a solid understanding of security and threat management, and protection issues involving business resources, risks and monitoring – as well as business continuity strategies.

Learning outcomes

On completion of this module, you’ll be able to:

  • identify and analyse critical security risks, threats and vulnerabilities in information systems, accounting for the current threat landscape.
  • gather and synthesise information from multiple sources to aid in the systematic analysis of risks and security issues.
  • critically determine appropriate methodologies, tools and techniques to mitigate and/or solve security risks and their business impact.
  • articulate the legal, social, ethical and professional issues faced by information security and risk professionals.
